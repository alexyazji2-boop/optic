## The conflicts, and how each was resolved

The rule throughout: live's behaviour stays wherever the overhaul changed it, with the rules on top.

1. **`app/paper.py`, the page's caveats (2750801).** Live added a "How fills are modelled" note (pitch audit,
   `EXECUTION_NOTE`) ending "No commission or slippage is charged", which the cost rule makes false for new
   trades. The note stays, without that sentence, and `execution_note(since)` adds the dated cost sentence: "Since
   <date>, each new trade is charged 5 basis points a side for a fund or 10 for a stock, plus 5 of slippage ...
   Trades opened before then carry no commission or slippage." Live's pitch-audit test now checks that.
2. **`static/v2/js/core/legal.js`, the Portfolio notice (2750801).** Live moved every page notice into one registry
   (`app/legal.py` `PAGE_AREAS`, copied word for word in `legal.js`, enforced by `test_legal_registry.py`). The new
   notice is in all three copies (registry, v2, Classic) and in `TRACKER_SIMULATED`. The branch's text said "Trades
   opened since 2026-10-21 carry a modelled allowance", which would be false for the 12 days of old-rule trades
   since then, so no copy names a date: "Since the rules change dated on this page, new trades carry a modelled
   allowance for commission and slippage; earlier trades carry none". `REGISTRY_VERSION` is now
   `disclosures-2026-11-02`. The notice is still the operator's wording, pending counsel, like the rest.
3. **`static/v2/js/pages/tracker.js`, the ledger and rules table (2750801).** Kept live's "Open positions last
   marked" line, the "at the scan's price" wording and the "Does not wait for" row; added the costs-aware Total
   return tile and the per-book rows (bullish only, the stop floor, Instruments, All positions together, Trading
   costs).
4. **`app/main.py`, startup (d5235c9).** Both jobs start: live's shortlist job and the research watch (still off
   unless `OPTIC_RESEARCH_BOOK` is set).
5. **`app/paper.py`, imports and caveats (d5235c9).** Both live's market-date clock (`session_mod`) and the greeks
   import; the caveats keep live's dated fill note and add the branch's three (one leg, in shares; exposure by what a
   position moves; the earnings cluster limit).
6. **`tracker.js`, the page's assembly (d5235c9, 2a4895b).** Live's empty-state wording and its portfolio scenarios
   stay; the research watch draws last, after the rules; `load` and `retry` serve both. The branch's keeping of open
   `<details>` folds across redraws sits beside live's section folds (`ui/sections.js`), which work by class and
   button, so the two do not overlap.

The branch's own test of the notice looked for single-quoted strings; live's `legal.js` uses double quotes, so the
test now reads either and checks all three copies equal the registry.

**How the books will behave.** Live's books on 2026-11-02, from `/api/tracker`, against the new 1x ceiling:

| Book | Open | Shorts among them | Held (3x funds at 3x) | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 6 | 2 | 0.53x | +2.68% |
| Balanced | 15 | 3 | 2.26x | -3.52% |
| Aggressive | 11 | 2 | 3.40x | -17.64% |

Open positions keep the rules they were opened with, shorts included, until their stop, target or the 45-day time
stop. Over the ceiling a book is never made to sell, so balanced and aggressive will open nothing until enough of
those positions close. Conservative, at 0.53x, is under it. Every new trade is long, in shares, sized at 0.25%, 0.5% or 1%
risk, with costs charged.

## What to set when it ships

* **The rules-changed date sets itself.** The first boot of this build stamps `rules:2026-10-21` in the ledger's
  `ledger_meta` with the boot time, and the pages print its New York date. Nothing names a date by hand. On the
  local check, with the 2026-10-21 preview's stamp removed from the copied ledger, it read "Rules changed on Nov 2,
  2026". After the deploy, check that `/api/tracker` gives `rules_change.date` equal to the deploy date. A later
  rollback to 769d911 is safe for the ledger: the new columns are added, never required.
* **`TRACKER_RISK_PCT`** is no longer read. If Railway sets it, remove it.
* **`OPTIC_RESEARCH_BOOK`** stays off unless the owner turns it on. On, it reads about a hundred daily-bar requests
  and the SEC's 8-K pages once a trading day after the close; it never calls a model.
* **Rebase first if live moves.** If origin/main has moved past 769d911, rebase this branch and run the full suite
  again before pushing. The pivots fix, which also moves stops, is already live (bb5cd670).
* **Nothing else changes on deploy.** No new paid call; `/api/research-book` reads the archive only.
