# Optic Portfolio: the books' rules from 2026-10-21

The owner asked on 2026-10-21 for the books' triggers to be reconsidered, because the aggressive book was down
about 8% in its first three weeks, and approved the five rules below the same day: "Build all five proposed
portfolio rules as recommended, keep open positions under their original rules, and keep the record running with
a dated rules-changed line."

## Why the aggressive book was down

Measured from the live ledger on 2026-10-21 (aggressive book, -$7,948, -7.95%):

* It was the balanced book's signal at twice the size. On the 19 positions both books took, aggressive lost
  $7,888 and balanced $4,048.
* The 13 positions opened on 2026-09-29 used the whole 25% risk budget in one session and lost $8,337. Everything
  opened later made $389. The per-scan limit did not pace it: scans run every four hours and anyone can start one.
* Nothing capped total position size, only risk at the stop. Gross exposure ran 1.75x to 3.2x equity (2.7x on
  average). SMH fell 7.2% over the period while SPY rose 1%.
* By leg: shorts -$4,751, option legs -$2,042 (each beside a share position on the same name), long shares
  -$1,155.
* `prefer_options` was read nowhere, so "options preferred" did nothing.

## The rules

Settings on each book in `app/paper.py`; any one can be switched back without code.

| Rule | Setting | Conservative | Balanced | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long only | `allow_shorts` | False | False | False |
| Positions at market value, at most | `max_gross_exposure` | no cap | 2x equity | 2x equity |
| Stop at least this many ATR14 from the entry | `min_stop_atr` | 1.5 | 1.5 | 1.5 |
| Options only on these IV verdicts | `option_iv_verdicts` | none (no options) | cheap | cheap |
| One position per idea, option preferred when allowed | `one_leg_per_idea`, `prefer_options` | yes, shares only | yes | yes |
| Trading costs | `charge_costs` | yes | yes | yes |

Costs are 5 basis points a side for a fund and 10 for a stock, plus 5 of slippage (the validation program's
convention), charged on both sides in the P&L. Option premium is charged the same rate, which understates a real
option spread.

## What stays the same

The entry signal (the swing composite at each book's bar), the risk per trade, the 2R target, the 45-day time stop
and the 86-name list. Optic's validation program found no entry signal with a reliable edge (0 of 82 rules
supported), so the newer strategies were not swapped in as triggers.

## Old positions and the record

* Positions opened before the change keep `rules_version` NULL and `cost_bps` NULL: their old rules and no costs.
* The change is dated in `ledger_meta` (`rules:2026-10-21`) the first time a build with these rules opens the
  ledger, after any restart. It is not a restart: the record keeps running, and both Portfolio pages show a
  "Rules changed on" line.
* Costs apply to new trades only. Charged on every trade up to 2026-10-21 they would have taken conservative from
  +1.60% to +1.11%, balanced from -1.97% to -3.39% and aggressive from -7.95% to -10.26%.

## Evidence

The replay (PT-1) ran the books' rules on 1993-2026 daily prices for 18 funds and 40 large stocks, with Optic's
own screen, chart score, support levels, regime and stage code, a pre-registered plan and decision rule, and three
dated addenda. With costs, the old rules lost in every period for every book, about as much as random entries did.
Long only helped every book in every period on the funds alone; the stop floor helped the balanced and aggressive
books; the 2x cap cut the worst drawdown in every period. Regime and stage gates, a correlation cap, per-session
pacing and a trailing exit were mixed or hurt once the books were long only. Even the best rule set trailed holding
SPY in two of three periods. A back-test describes the past and is not a forecast.

The option rule rests on the validation program's IV study (observed option data, 2024-10 to 2026-10): on every
verdict but "cheap" the next month moved less than the premium priced.

## Revised the same day (commit d5235c9), after the research brief of 2026-10-21

The owner's research brief asked for these repairs to be audited and said "cheap IV must not automatically cause
Optic to buy calls instead of shares". Changes, all settings on each book:

| Change | Setting | Conservative | Balanced | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Options paused until an option implementation is validated | `option_iv_verdicts`, `prefer_options` | none (as before) | none, shares | none, shares |
| Every book has an exposure ceiling | `max_gross_exposure` | 1x | 2x | 2x |
| Exposure counts what a position moves | `LEVERAGED`, `mark_delta` | 3x funds at 3x, options at delta | same | same |
| At most N positions reporting within 3 days of one another | `max_event_cluster` | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Over a limit | `LIMIT_POLICY` | stop adding, never sell to fit | same | same |

Recommended and not built (owner's decision): risk per trade 0.25%, 0.5% and 1%, every book at most 1x. On the
replay this roughly halved the worst drawdowns at similar or better return-to-drawdown.

The research behind these, and the forward research watch, is in `research-2026-10-21/REPORT.md`.
