Implied vol is **1.54x realized** [[src cases]]. At-the-money IVs run 24.1% to 25.3% on the Oct 16 expiry and 28.53% on the Nov 13 call [[src greeks]], against 20-day realized vol of 16.5% and 10-day of 19.5% [[src technicals]]. Realized vol is contracting. Nov 13 carries a premium for earnings on Nov 2. There is no IV rank (where today's IV sits against its own past year), so "high" is only relative to realized. OTM call IV (25.33%) sits above OTM put IV (23.98%) [[src flow]]. Positive net GEX of +$797.053m per 1% leans toward ranges holding [[src gex]], which is the setting Optic's regime note says favours premium selling.

What argues against it:
- IV above realized is the usual state, so it is not a free edge. It is what sellers are paid for taking tail risk.
- CPI Wednesday, PPI Thursday, monthly expiry Friday [[src whats_next]]. Earnings on Nov 2 means selling through it collects the post-print IV drop and takes the gap. Apple has beaten 8 of 8, and the data here has no price-reaction history.
- Price sits near the 52-week high of 345.34 on a stacked-bullish trend, which is bad for short calls. Short puts face the 323.89 flip point, 3.786% below, where dealer hedging starts adding to moves.
- Selling premium has a capped gain and a large possible loss unless it is defined-risk.

The read would change if 10-day realized vol approaches IV, or on a close below 323.89.
