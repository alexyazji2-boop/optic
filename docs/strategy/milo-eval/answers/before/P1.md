Two things drive concentration, and the headline position size is only one of them. The first is the largest single weight: if one name is a third of the book, a bad week in that name is a bad week in the whole book. The second is shared risk, the part people miss. Five positions that all trade as "mega-cap tech" behave like one position of five times the size when the group sells off, so the count of tickers can look diversified while the exposure isn't. Sector is the first thing to check for that, and then whether the names tend to move together on macro news.
