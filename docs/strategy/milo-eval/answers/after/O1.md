Even at 0.30, delta is only a rough, model-based guide to the chance the option finishes in the money (above 360 at expiry). Finishing in the money isn't the same as paying off. You also have to clear the premium: at the mid price of **$3.53** [[src strategy_ideas]], breakeven is 360 + 3.53 = 363.53. That's about 6.6% above the 340.97 last close [[src quote]] (363.53 divided by 340.97). So the chance of a profit at expiry is lower than the delta suggests.

On the cost side, the spread between bid and ask is **9.93%** of the price (3.35 bid, 3.70 ask), with open interest of 3,751 and 685 contracts traded today [[src strategy_ideas]]. That's a real toll to pay on the way in. Earnings land on Nov 2, inside this expiry, and Optic's own note is that implied volatility (the market's price for movement) tends to collapse after the print, which can hurt a long call even when the direction is right [[src news]].

My read: treat delta as a "how far out of the money is this" label, not as odds of making money. Prices are delayed, and the overnight print is 341.61, so these figures may have moved since the close.
