## Errors by case

Recurring: several answers deny data that was loaded (own-history P/E in B1-A, B2-B, L1-A, F1-B; conviction 74 in L2-B; portfolio in P1-A) and substitute memory or a different question. Neither version flags the earnings-catalyst tag on a Q3-recap headline except where asked (L2-A, A1, B3-B).

### B1
- **A:** "Apple's own multiple history isn't in what I have loaded, but from memory it spent most of the last decade well below 39." Wrong: the data included Apple's 4-year P/E median 28.2 (range 26-32, 100th percentile). It denies loaded data and substitutes unsourced recall.
- **A:** "PEG of 2.69", "MACD has slipped under its signal line", "composite" are left undefined for a beginner on adaptive (clarity/adaptation, not a factual error).

### B2
- **B:** "Apple's own past P/E isn't loaded here. From memory, though, it has spent most of the last ten years below 39." Wrong: the 4-year median 28.2 was in the data; the key check (compare to own history) is answered from memory instead.
- **B:** "the broad market has usually traded well below that too" is an unsourced market comparison presented as fact.
- **A:** "CONTEXT has no P/E for similar companies" leaks an internal prompt label to the user (presentation flaw, not a factual error).

### B3
- **A:** "Apple printed $341.37 in after-hours". The data's after-hours figure was $341.34 (small but a figure error).
- **A:** "no Apple-specific news of substance is in the feed". Answer B cites an in-session item (Tim Cook meeting China's vice premier); A's blanket statement overstates the absence of stock-specific news.

### D1
- **B:** "That cluster has to give way ON A CLOSING BASIS before the $216.58 to $214.57 area is in play." Wrong for a day trade: price can trade intraday to 214-216 without any daily close below 222; it also reads as a rule, not data.
- **B:** "ATR ... is $5.59, so a normal day can cover that whole stack and still not clear it." Self-contradictory: a $5.59 range exceeds the $4.44 stack, so "cover it and not clear it" is muddled.
- **A:** "the flip point (206.06) is about 9% away, so it says little about the first hour". Muddled: distance to the flip does not reduce the relevance of the current positive-gamma regime; it only means a regime change is unlikely.
- **A:** "CONTEXT has no option bid-ask data" leaks an internal label.

### D2
- **A:** "That spans 225 to 230 on its own" (of ATR $5.59). ATR is a range size, not a location; it does not map onto a specific 225-230 band.
- **A:** "A move through the 222.4 to 222.9 cluster ... would strain the damping story." Moving-average/Fibonacci levels are not GEX levels; the gamma model's regime would not change there (flip is 206.06).

### S1
- **A:** "Optic flags that the chart is mostly measuring the period before a fresh earnings headline, so its trend reading carries half its usual weight." Garbled reasoning, and it passes on the reweighting without noting the trigger was a Q3-recap headline tagged as an earnings catalyst, not an actual report.
- **A:** "a breakout through 345 to 350 would need a catalyst to overcome it" states a dealer-hedging model output as near-mechanical fact (the GEX sign is an assumption, which A noted one sentence earlier).
- **B:** "rallies into the wall tend to stall and breakouts need a catalyst" is the same overreach, though B prefixes it with "Under the model's assumption".

### S2
- **A and B:** both repeat that the chart weight was halved by "a headline tagged earnings" without flagging that the headline was a quarter-in-review piece, so the reweighting itself is questionable (minor; it is an Optic input, reported accurately).
