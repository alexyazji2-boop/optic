## Errors

**B1 A**: "Apple's own multiple history isn't in what I have loaded, but from memory it spent most of the last decade well below 39." A recalled claim used as the valuation anchor in place of data; honest that it is memory, but still unsourced evidence. Also "Eight beats in a row is the reason a 39x multiple holds" asserts a cause the data does not establish.

**B1 B**: "Revenue growth is **16.4%**" with no period; that is latest-quarter y/y, while last fiscal year was +6.4%, so a beginner reads it as the annual rate. The "$5 trillion market cap" aside is unsourced filler.

**B2 A**: "a P/E tells you nothing about how Apple compares with other companies." Taken literally this is wrong (P/E is routinely used cross-sectionally); the intended point is that the own-history comparison says nothing about peers.

**B2 B**: "From memory, though, it has spent most of the last ten years below 39, and the broad market has usually traded well below that too." Recalled figures presented as the answer to "is it high", in place of data.

**D1 A**: "CPI and options expiry are Wednesday." Another answer shows the calendar lists an Oct 14 expiry, so this may be sourced, but it omits the larger Friday Oct 16 monthly expiry, which matters more for a short-horizon NVDA plan. Also "the next open is ... about eight hours away" from a ~00:00 ET snapshot is about 9.5 hours.

**D1 B**: "ATR ... is $5.59, so a normal day can cover that whole stack and still not clear it." Muddled: a $4.44 stack inside a $5.59 average range says reach is possible, not that it won't clear; and from a $224.23 open the stack top is $6.62 away, more than one ATR.

**D2 A**: "$230 has the largest net GEX within about 2.3%." Confusing: $230 is 1.6% above spot. Also says the nearest chain series holds 2.1% of gamma yet calls Fri Oct 16 the "nearer test" without reconciling that the monthly seems absent from the chain.

**D2 B**: "The nearest listed expiry, Oct 19, carries only 2.1% of total gamma." Oct 19 2026 is a Monday; the October monthly is Fri Oct 16. The same date recurs in O2 B, so it likely comes from the data, but neither answer flags that the Fri Oct 16 monthly is missing from the chain, which matters for a pin question.

**S1 A**: "the discount may be conservative." Minor: halving the chart weight because a recap headline was tagged as earnings is a mis-tag, not conservatism; the right point is that the reweighting is spurious. No factual errors otherwise.

**S1 B**: "Under the model's assumption that means rallies into the wall tend to stall and breakouts need a catalyst." Overstates a modelled, unobserved dealer position into a behavioral rule.

**S2 A**: "IV (the market's price for movement) is likely to stay elevated into the print." Asserted without any figure; the other version shows the data had the IV term structure to support or test it.

**L1 A**: "From memory it sat below 39 for most of the last decade, so by its own standards this is a stretched entry." A recalled history used as the valuation verdict, not data. Also "if gross margin slips below 46.9%" uses the current level as a threshold, so any dip trips it.

Not having the figure is honest, but "correcting" the user and substituting the short-term composite is a reasoning error that leaves the question unanswered.
