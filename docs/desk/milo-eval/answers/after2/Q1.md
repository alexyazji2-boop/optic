My read: memory is a cyclical business, and today's margin is the exception rather than the baseline.

**The price.** Trailing P/E (price over the last year's earnings) is **12.8x** and forward P/E is **4.6x** [[src research]]. A multiple that low can mean the market doubts the earnings will last, not that anyone is giving shares away. I checked the decimal point too. Whether it's cheap against Micron's own history isn't computed here, so that half of your question stays open.

**Bottom line.** Strong business, unresolved price. What would change the read: the December 23 report (consensus EPS $38.21) showing margins holding, and free cash flow catching up with profit. Still not advice, just what the data can and can't tell you.
