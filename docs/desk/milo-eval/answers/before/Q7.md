**I can't tell you whether JPMorgan beat, and the Desk is built so it can't call it either.**

Two reasons. First, this Desk page is about economic reports, like the inflation number due tomorrow. JPMorgan's results aren't on it, so I have no figures for the bank.

Second, "beat" means better than what analysts predicted. The Desk says it carries no such predictions, because they're sold under licence. It only compares each report with its own last reading. Think of a runner's time: without the coach's predicted time, you know how fast they ran, but not whether it was good. Why it matters if you hold the stock: a company can post a strong result and still fall if the guess was higher, so a result alone doesn't tell you where the price goes.

So I won't guess at a yes or no. That would be a number I don't have.
