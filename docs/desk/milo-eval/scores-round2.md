## Errors by case

**Q1**
- A: "Net debt is $5.64B against $54.165B of equity (debt-to-equity 0.28)". 0.28 is not net debt over equity (about 0.10), so it mixes gross and net debt without saying so.
- A: It leaves out capex and free cash flow ($15.86bn capex, $1.67bn FCF), the main thing that tempers the earnings case.
- A: "Over 21 sessions the stock trails its sector fund by 3.36 points". This is price performance, which adds little to a long-term business case.
- B: "$37.38bn of revenue at a 22.8% margin". The margin type isn't labelled; operating margin was 26.1%, so a reader could misread it.
- B: "The stock is 24% below its $1,255 June high" and "consensus EPS $38.21". Both are given with no source.
- B: "I checked the decimal point too." This is filler with nothing behind it.

**Q2**
- A: There's no event volatility from options, even though it was in context, and no explicit "what would change the read".
- A: The RSI, support and 200-day detail is chart material that's off-focus on an Earnings page.
- B: "Analysts expect EPS of $1.10, against $1.21 last quarter". This compares the estimate with last quarter's actual; it leaves out the revenue consensus, the $1.01-$1.25 range and revisions.
- B: "33.5% on the Oct 23 expiry ... 4.4% of spot". That straddle covers about eight sessions including CPI, not just the report, and the answer doesn't say so.
- B: It never says outright that guidance and management commentary are missing from the free data; it only says "The data doesn't say".

**Q3**
- A: "CPI and an options expiry land on the same day (Oct 14)". There's no 8:30 ET time, so the pre-open shock isn't placed. There's also no VWAP.
- B: "Day low | 223.0602". The false precision adds nothing; otherwise no factual errors found.

**Q4**
- A: "Tuesday was a record high at lunchtime". No cited data supports an intraday time; this is invented detail.
- A: "so the group carried it". AAPL lagging its sector means the sector outran AAPL, not that it carried AAPL.
- B: "sits beside the 50-day (323.79)". This conflicts with the 50-day EMA of $327.01 given earlier, and the SMA/EMA switch isn't labelled.
- B: "The market cap is $4.98 trillion, and the $5 trillion door stays shut". This is unsourced and has no bearing on a swing trade.
- Both: Neither covers the broad market (SPY flat, VIX 15.3, 10-year at 5.2%); they stop at sector relative performance.

**Q5**
- A: "prices are up 3.71% ... against the Fed's 2% aim". The Fed's 2% target is defined on PCE, not CPI.
- A: "Oil is at $95.28". No source is given.
- A: It misses that the 10-year fell 2.3bp today while tech fell, which is evidence against rates driving this week's drop.
- B: There are no yields at all, so the mechanism is never checked against today's data. The y/y figure (3.71%) is missing too.
- B: "Optic's read of the whole market is mixed ... (score 2.8)". An unexplained composite score is jargon at the simple setting.

**Q6**
- A: "the Desk piece here doesn't say it". This is honest about its context, but it gives no test, no counter-case and no figures, so it's of little use.
- B: "The claim would fail if RSP's lead over SPY shrank to nothing over the next five sessions". A future window can't falsify a claim about the past five sessions.
- B: It leaves out breadth (5 of 11 sector funds rose), which is direct evidence on how widely the softness spread.

**Q7**
- A: It doesn't explain why the two consensus figures differ (calendar vs history table, likely different vintages); it only says which is better is unknown.
- A: "the next big test for bank stocks is tomorrow's inflation report". This overlooks BAC's results the same morning, a more direct test.
