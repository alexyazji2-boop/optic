**Three predictions, each with its own confidence**
- **Results, low to moderate:** better than expected. Consensus is **$1.98** EPS and **$113.61bn** revenue for the quarter ending about September [[src framework]]. Eight straight beats make a beat more tradition than surprise.
- **Guidance, low:** likely to support estimates, since last quarter's operating margin was 2.6 points above a year earlier. Company guidance text is not in Optic's data, so this is inference, separate from analyst consensus.
- **Reaction, low:** two-sided. After past beats the stock moved -1.5% on average in the reacting session, so a likely beat is not a bullish price call.

**The hurdle looks balanced.** The P/E is in the top fifth of its five-year range [[src framework]], so the price assumes things keep going well. Against that, the +10.9% consensus growth already assumes a slowdown, revisions are flat and there was no run-up: +2.5% over 21 sessions against +2.3% for SPY. The beat record is EPS only; past revenue surprises are not in the data.

**News** adds little: a JPMorgan target reset and a Siri-subscription estimate that appears twice but is one story (both analyst opinion), plus reporting on a HomeView hub that bears on neither the quarter nor guidance.

**Cases.** Base: a beat, supportive guidance, a move inside the priced 4.42%. Bull: a clear beat plus guidance above the 2.91 next-quarter consensus [[src framework]], enough to exceed the priced range upward. Bear: a smaller-than-usual beat or a guide below consensus; with expectations balanced, the stock could fall on a headline beat. Deciders: revenue against $113.6bn, EPS against 1.98, the guide against 2.91.

**What would invalidate it:** an EPS surprise below zero, guidance below consensus, or a first reaction beyond the priced edges against the lean. A right call can still lose in options: a bought option pays the expected 4.4% up front and loses value as volatility drops after the release, so it needs a bigger move, the right way, before expiry.
