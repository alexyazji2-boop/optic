$RIVN is a turnaround bet rather than an established business: the numbers are moving the right way, but it still loses money on every line that matters and has paid for it by issuing shares. Whether that's investable is a risk call, and it's yours; here's what the data supports.

**What's improving.** Revenue was **$5.88bn** over the four quarters to 2026-06-30, and the latest quarter grew **27.2%** on a year earlier [[src research]] (company statements). Gross profit turned positive in 2025, $144M after a $1.2bn loss in 2024 [[src company]], and the latest quarter's operating margin was -50.4%, up 13.4 points on the quarter before [[src earnings_momentum]].

**What isn't.** A 2.7% gross margin means the cars roughly pay for themselves and nothing else, and the "nothing else" is where the **-$3.23bn** trailing net loss lives [[src research]]. In 2025 operating cash flow was -$0.78bn and capex $1.71bn, so free cash flow was **-$2.49bn**, and the diluted share count rose **17.08%**. Cash was $3.58bn against $6.65bn of debt at the end of 2025 [[src company]]. At 2025's burn that cash covers about 1.4 years (3.58 / 2.49). That's arithmetic, not a forecast: the balance sheet is nine months old and I can't see what's been raised or spent since.

**Price.** Market value is $18.67bn, about 3.2x trailing revenue (18.67 / 5.88) [[src research]]. The forward P/E of -7.43 is negative because earnings are, so it can't separate cheap from dear. Optic doesn't compute Rivian's valuation against its own history or peers here, so "is the price right" is the part I can't answer.

**What could go wrong.** Another equity raise would dilute holders again. 14.9% of the float is short at 8.0 days to cover [[src company]], which cuts both ways. The report on Oct 29, after the close, is the next look at cash and margins.

**Bottom line.** My read: promising trajectory, unproven funding. A gross margin that holds above zero, operating cash flow turning positive and slower share-count growth would each strengthen the case; none is in the data yet.
