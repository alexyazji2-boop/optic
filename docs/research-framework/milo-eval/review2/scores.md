At 26 July 2026 NVDA had $22.4bn cash plus $34.1bn current marketable securities against $33.4bn long-term debt, so about $23bn of net cash, not net debt. The conclusion ("balance sheet isn't the worry") holds, but both figures are wrong.
- A: "Whether 28x is rich for this company needs its own multiple history, which Optic doesn't compute here." This is an honest can't-see, but it leaves out the key valuation fact that the P/E is low in its own five-year range. Scored as an evidence cost, not an accuracy one.
- A: there is no returns-on-capital-vs-cost-of-capital evidence, and the bull case is only implied. Thin for a long judgement question.
- B: no balance sheet or net cash anywhere, though the "must" list asks for it.
- B: "a price that screens cheap against its own history and peers". The answer shows no peer evidence, so "and peers" has no support on the page.
- B: the "Where to reassess" table, with levels conditional on results, reads close to buy zones in a long-term question. B labels them "not targets", so this is a mild risk.
