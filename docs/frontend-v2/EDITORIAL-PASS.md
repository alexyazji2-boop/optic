# Editorial, interaction and credibility pass (2026-10-19 to 20)

This pass covers v2 in `static/v2` and two server fixes. Nothing was deployed. The passing tests do not make the product polished; the remaining issues below are real.

## How it was checked
- **Two read-only audits** of every page's source: a copy audit with about 80 items, and a credibility audit of zero defaults, conclusions drawn from missing data, clocks, formats and cross-ticker state.
- **Real screens** on the local server (real Yahoo data, signed in to the demo account) and on the live site, captured at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- **Difficult states made in the test browser only:** the server is never changed. Each one is made by rewriting, failing or holding a single API answer (`~/Optic/work/v2-editorial/kit.py`):
  - a slow load;
  - a failed quote, ticker and session, then Try again;
  - a partial short-interest record;
  - Milo switched off;
  - a failed Milo answer;
  - the real Markets brief from the live site.
- **Scripts**, all in `~/Optic/work/v2-editorial/`:
  - `capture.py`: before and after screens;
  - `states.py`: each state and whether its control works;
  - `transitions.py`: ticker changes with the destination's data held back 2.5 s, plus the route against the sidebar and tab state;
  - `milo_context.py`: what each Milo request actually carried;
  - `wraps.py`: controls whose text wraps, at five widths, signed in and as a guest.

## Completed fixes

### Copy
- **Development commentary removed:**
  - "the current app" became "the classic app" in the 15 places where a feature still lives there. v2 is the default now, so "current" pointed the wrong way.
  - "paid model", "on this deployment", "after a restart" and "the server's next pass" became plain descriptions.
- **Findings lead, with their period:**
  - Home's next-session range.
  - The Overview's history ratio: "Options price ±7.0% through Oct 24, against a typical ±3.9% for the same period".
  - The 13F holder sentence.
  - The filings count.
  - Discover's sector, cross-asset and screen lines.
  - Scan's empty and stale results.
- **Formulas folded behind "How this works":**
  - Home's ÷ 16 and ÷ 1.6.
  - Macro's expected move and the rotation quadrant definitions.
  - The Overview's ratio arithmetic and trend-score inputs.
  - Options' payoff-line model note, Black-Scholes teaching note, contracts mechanics and dealer gamma conventions.
  - News' tone thresholds.
  - Fear and Greed's scale.
- **Options group lines** now say what is an estimate ("dealer gamma is estimated from open interest, not reported positions; flow is the day's options volume, not a record of trades") and what is model output ("not recommendations").
- **Discover:** "A reading of the three sections below, not a fourth thing to read" is removed. The heading says it.
- **The Markets brief's `**bold**` markers** render as bold. The text is escaped first, and a hyphen before a figure becomes the minus sign.
- **Dates in server prose** ("2026-11-02") read as Optic writes them ("Nov 2, 2026"). Addresses, queries and ids are left alone.

### Milo
- **Global entry point:** the top bar's Ask Milo, with Scout, is the one gold entry.
- **Contextual actions:** these are quiet buttons with Milo's line icon, at the right of their section's heading.
  - Options panels used to put theirs at the panel's foot. It now moves into the heading row and names the panel in the request, for example "AAPL Dossier, Options: What options are pricing".
  - The Chart side rail's Ask Milo wrapped onto two lines. It is now its icon alone, named for screen readers.
- **Generated text is labelled** in one form of words. Model-written prose says "Written by an AI model from the figures, N ago. It interprets them and can be wrong; the figures are the record." This covers:
  - the Read;
  - the Desk;
  - the release read;
  - the weekly update;
  - the sector read;
  - the pre-earnings brief.

  A Desk edition built by fixed rules says "Compiled by fixed rules from the figures; no model wrote it." Every Milo answer says "Generated by an AI model from Optic's data", and watchlist changes say they are computed, not written.
- **Context checked by sending real requests** on the stand-in model. The ticker, view and label were right from:
  - AAPL Options, through a panel button and through the top bar;
  - MSFT after AAPL, which carries `previous_ticker` AAPL;
  - a linked Home panel, which carries NVDA;
  - Compare, which carries no ticker;
  - an instrument page;
  - Chart's Explain, which carries the range and interval.

  An instrument is now named ("US 10Y (^TNX)"), not just its symbol.
- **Unavailable and failed states:**
  - When Milo is switched off, it says so and disables Send.
  - When an answer fails, the panel says "Milo did not respond (HTTP N). Wait a moment and send it again; your question is still in the box", and the question is put back in the box.

### Credibility
- **Missing data is no longer a zero:**
  - DCF upside, discount rate and terminal share;
  - institutional and insider holdings, and the holder bars;
  - short-interest share of float;
  - at-the-money IV, which was halved when one side was missing;
  - unusual-contract IV;
  - ITM probability;
  - a Home contract's cost;
  - Compare's "rose 0%";
  - the sector board's counts;
  - the Overview's "valuation added 0".
- **No conclusions from missing data:**
  - "Squeeze potential low" with no share of float. The server now says "not known", so Classic prints the honest word too.
  - The Earnings "Missed by 0.0%" with no result, and a "cheap" pill for an unknown stance.
  - The margin sentence when one growth figure was missing.
  - The Overview's "Not graded" or "No report date yet" when the source had failed. It now says "Not available right now", with the reason.
  - Chart read's "below" for a missing position, "not stacked" for missing structure, and "Neutral" for an unknown RSI state.
  - An up or down colour on a missing figure, in about 18 places.
- **Clocks:**
  - The header now says what its price is. In session it is a delayed trade ("Delayed about 15 minutes, last trade 3:45 PM ET"), never the previous close. Outside it, it is "Close of Mon Oct 19". An extended print is said apart ("Overnight 343.21 +0.08% at 11:58 PM ET").
  - Home's panel times carry their zone.
  - The Read separates "Prices and headlines updated N ago" from when the text was written.
  - "Invalid Date ET" can no longer appear.
- **The header price no longer steps back:** the later of the two quotes (20-second quote, 2-minute ticker snapshot) wins.
- **No figures under the wrong ticker:**
  - Chart's ticker data, Earnings' late errors and briefs, and Compare's results stay with the ticker they were asked for. Compare's newest request wins.
  - The probe found no wrong-ticker frame in four slowed transitions.
- **Overview's Try again** used to turn the page into "[object Object]". It now re-renders the page.
- **Route state:** the sidebar and tabs matched the route on 18 routes. Instrument pages now mark Markets.
- **Sign in** never wraps.

### Formats
- **Signs:** a value that rounds to zero carries no sign ("0.00%", not "−0.00%").
- **Units and values:**
  - `compact(999,950)` reads 1.0M, not 1,000.0K.
  - Blank strings, booleans and arrays are not numbers.
  - A bad date prints nothing, not "undefined NaN".
- **Ordinals:** 81st and 22nd, not 81th and 22th.
- **Missing values:** "n/a" is never capitalised into a headline ("N/a").
- **Splits** read "3 for 2" and "1 for 10, a reverse split". They used to read "2 for 1" and "0 for 1".
- **Precision:** the implied move is 2 decimals everywhere, and multi-year returns and drawdowns are 1 decimal on both the Overview and Investing.
- **Tables align by what each column holds** (`ui/align.js`): figures right, words and years left, on every page.
- **Panels:** a failure inside a panel is no longer a card inside a card.

### Interaction checks (all passed)
- **Loading:** labelled placeholders and a status line.
- **Failure:** each failed source has its own Try again, and the page recovers.
- **Partial data:** short interest shows "Not reported" and no reading.
- **Keyboard:**
  - Focus moves into the Milo drawer, and Escape returns it to the opener.
  - A visible 2px focus ring on buttons and tabs.
- **Phone:** no page overflow on Financials at 390, all 8 tables scroll inside their own boxes, and the top bar stays stuck.

## Remaining issues
1. **Copy:** about 30 lower-traffic items from the audit are not rewritten. These include the Positions tracker's long lead, the Brokerage and Paper Desk notes, some Scan and Insiders method lines, and Milo's action-card messages. Server-written prose is untouched, apart from its dates and minus signs.
2. **Formulas still inline:** a few remain in panels this pass did not reach: the Investing scenario notes, Home's Market Overview panel subtitles, and Compare's "SPY ≈ 0.65".
3. **Hard-coded claims not yet sourced:** "about a third of the time" (implied volatility), "Bullish at +30" beside a 0 to 100 composite, and "+12 / −12" (Fear and Greed). They are folded or unchanged, not verified.
4. **Mismatched beat-rate denominators:** Earnings counts quarters with a surprise, while Financials counts all quarters. The server should send one `surprise_count`.
5. **Chart gaps:** charts still bridge missing points by index, and missing open, high or low on weekly bars are filled from the close. The values are honest but the drawing smooths over the gaps.
6. **Dates:** several date formats remain (`fullDate`, `shortDate`, `longDate`, `etDay`, `etStamp`), and some ad hoc forms ("Jun 30 25") in Financials' segment table.
7. **Precision still differs** for the fair value range (0 decimals on the Overview, 2 on Investing) and some percentage-point labels ("pp" in one place, "%" in another).
8. **Phone tabs:** the Options and Read group tabs wrap to two lines at 390. This is deliberate and legible, but it is a wrap.
9. **Live data:** the local server runs on a free Yahoo feed outside market hours. Option chains, the in-session clock wording and live Milo answers (a real model, not the stand-in) were not seen populated in this pass.
10. **Classic** keeps its own copy and formats. Only the two server fixes reach it.
