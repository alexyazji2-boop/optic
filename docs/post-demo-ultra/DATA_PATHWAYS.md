# Data pathways and caches (Phase 09)

What this is: every financial-data pathway in Optic, traced provider to API to normalisation to cache to calculation
to front end to Milo's context, and every cache with its key, lifetime, stale behaviour and auth boundary (directive
09, sections 1 and 7; R09-01, R09-07).

How it was made, and how far to trust it:
- **The audit:** a read-only agent audited the tree as it stood at 01:59 ET on 2026-10-30 (HEAD `0268f7f` plus that
  morning's uncommitted Phase 09 edits). It wrote the two parts below, every row cited to file:line, as drafts.
- **What was checked by hand:** eleven of its twenty RISK rows (H3, S02, S08, S10, S29, S30, S31, S33, B01, B04,
  B11), and four of its pathway claims. Those are the dividend carry priced as zero, the quote's currency defaulting
  to USD, the brokerage's `price_source` guard that nothing sets, and the `^TNX` units. All held, with one
  qualification: B01 is narrower than it reads, because signing in or out through the app already clears the
  browser's whole API cache (`core/store.js` `signIn`, `signOut`).
- **What was not checked:** the other rows stand as the agent wrote them. Line numbers have moved since, so search
  for the function name beside each one.

## 9. Metrics in the brief that are not supported at all

- **Real-time quotes**: only when `TRADIER_ACCESS_TOKEN` is set; otherwise every price is Yahoo's, delayed by the feed's own stated minutes (0 to 15). No other real-time source exists.
- **Session (daily-reset) VWAP**: not on any panel or the chart; only anchored VWAP (`indicators.py:69-85`) and the ORB backtest's internal one.
- **Dividend-adjusted (total-return) price history**: not on charts, returns, relative performance or backtests (`auto_adjust=False`, `yf.py:1293,1331,1392`); only pairs and the retirement tool read `Adj Close`.
- **Currency conversion** of quotes, market caps, statements, valuation or charts: not supported; only brokerage totals are converted (P31). Dividend currency is never stated (`main.py:8105`).
- **Corporate actions other than splits and dividends** (mergers, spin-offs, ticker changes, delistings): not modelled.
- **Macro consensus or forecast values**: not supported (`fred.py:206`); reported revenue against its estimate for a just-released report only from Optic's own pre-release record (`earnings_result.py:194-222`), else absent.
- **Option bid/ask timestamps and intraday open interest** from Yahoo: not provided; the flow signal is a volume and open-interest proxy with no trade tape.
- **Non-USD SEC filers** in revenue, EPS, P/E history and valuation: excluded (USD units only).

## F. RISK register (every RISK verdict above, one line each)

1. H3 `app/main.py:8453-8465`: reader-specific API JSON has no `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and no `Vary: Cookie`; only `/api/auth/me` (`auth/routes.py:161`) and the admin routes do.
2. S01 `app/providers/yf.py:79,155-164`: the adapter cache has no eviction, one lock per key ever seen, and caller-chosen key parts (ticker path, `period`, `range`). **Fixed in Phase 10:** entries no reader can be served again (older than a day and five minutes) are swept once a minute, the cache holds at most `CACHE_MAX` entries (20,000, oldest first), a key's lock goes with its entry, and the trendline and indicator routes take only the known periods (`_DAILY_PERIODS`).
3. S02 `app/providers/yf.py:443-470,1290,1307,1330-1367`: stand-in frames up to 6 h old are served with no age or flag, and a board can mix fresh and stand-in frames.
4. S03 `app/providers/yf.py:842-849`: a refused v7 quote document is cached as `None` for 30 s (the summary document is protected, `:839`), producing bar-close prices and missing ratios.
5. S07 `app/providers/yf.py:1496-1515`: a chain missing expiries whose call failed is cached 60 s; missing volume and open interest become 0 (`:1567-1568`).
6. S08 `app/providers/yf.py:1824-1826,1845-1849,2006,2035,2054,2072`: estimates, analyst view, insiders and institutions swallow every exception, 429 included, and cache the empty answer 1 to 6 h.
7. S10 `app/providers/yf.py:2099,2148`: the news cache key has no `limit` (callers ask for 12 and 36), so the first caller fixes the list size for 10 min; `_NEWS_GOOD` is unbounded.
8. S11 `yfinance/data.py:455-457`, `search.py:102`: the library's 64-entry LRU has no expiry, so the news search (and statements within a UTC day) can be answered from memory past Optic's TTLs (unverified live).
9. S13 `app/providers/tradier.py:106-133`: the Tradier overlay mutates the Yahoo cache's own dict (latent, only with `TRADIER_ACCESS_TOKEN`).
10. S16 `app/feeds.py:535-649`: no negative cache, so dead FRED, BLS or RSS hosts are asked again on every call and a failed refresh discards the stale copy. **Fixed in Phase 10:** a host that failed, refused (403, 429) or erred (5xx) is not asked again for `HOST_DOWN_SECONDS` (60 s); the next call fails at once the same way, and a caller that keeps a copy (`cached_json`) can still serve it.
11. S19 `app/universe.py:240-259,388-397`: the lock is held across two 25 s downloads and a failed day is retried on every search (including Milo's symbol check). **Fixed in Phase 10:** a failed refresh is not retried for `REFRESH_RETRY_SECONDS` (15 minutes), and the last good copy stands in meanwhile, marked stale; the lock still spans the once-a-day download itself.
12. S26 `app/ai.py:2075-2093,2261-2300`: written earnings briefs and sector reads are served to every reader for 6 h and 1 h under a key that is not tied to the facts they quote.
13. S29 `app/analytics/watchlist.py:66-82,241`: a failed pull is cached as `{}` for 90 s and a row's price (a daily-bar close) carries no timestamp.
14. S30 `app/analytics/relperf.py:87-96`: an empty failed download is cached for 90 minutes.
